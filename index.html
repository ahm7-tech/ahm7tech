Help & Support

Answers to the most common questions.

💰 How do I top up my wallet?

Sign in, open Add Funds, type the amount in USD and pick a payment method — Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, USDT, Binance or Bybit. Pay the exact amount shown. Most payments are found and credited automatically within a few minutes. If it is not found, upload a screenshot of your payment and the admin will approve it.

🛒 How do I buy a product?

Pick a product, choose the quantity and pay with your wallet balance — or pay directly with Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, USDT, Binance or Bybit. Your product details appear on this page right after the payment is confirmed, and a copy is also emailed to you. You can also find them later on the My Orders page.

⏱️ How long does delivery take?

Usually instantly, or a few minutes after your payment is confirmed. Check the My Orders page — if your order is still waiting, contact support with your order ID.

↩️ Is there a refund or replacement policy?

No refunds — please read the product description before buying. If something is wrong with the item you received, contact support and we will replace it where possible.

⚠️ How do I report a problem?

Message us on Telegram @ahm7storebot with your order ID and a screenshot of the problem, and we will help you.

💳 What payments are accepted?

Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, USDT, Binance and Bybit. You can pay per order, or top up your wallet once and buy in one tap.
